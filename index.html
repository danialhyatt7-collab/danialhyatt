Questions we get asked locally

Do you work with businesses in Islamabad and Rawalpindi?

Yes. We are based in Islamabad and work across both cities, including Blue Area, the F and G sectors, DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg Greens and Saddar. We can meet in person in the twin cities, or run the whole project over WhatsApp and calls.

How much does a website cost in Pakistan?

It depends on how many pages you need and whether you are selling online. Message us on WhatsApp with what you have in mind and we will send a clear quote in PKR, with no hidden extras.

How long does a website take?

You see a first design within about 7 days. Most sites go live within a few weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back to us.

Can you make my site show up on Google for my city?

Yes. Every site we build ships SEO-ready and loads in under two seconds on average. For local search we also set up your Google Business Profile, local business markup and city-specific pages so people searching in Islamabad or Rawalpindi can find you.

Do you take payment in Pakistani Rupees?

Yes, we quote and invoice in PKR for local clients, with bank transfer or the usual local payment methods.
