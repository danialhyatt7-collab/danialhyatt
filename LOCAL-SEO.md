# Islamabad local SEO plan — Designs Redefined

The site changes in this repo are the on-page half. They are necessary and they are
not sufficient: in Pakistan, local search for "web design islamabad" is won in the
Google map pack, and the map pack is driven by a Google Business Profile with real
reviews. Nothing on this list matters more than items 1 and 2.

## What is already done (in this repo)

- `LocalBusiness` / `ProfessionalService` JSON-LD on the homepage with phone, email,
  hours, geo coordinates, service catalogue and `areaServed` for Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
- `FAQPage` JSON-LD plus a matching visible FAQ (Google only trusts markup it can see).
- City landing pages at `/islamabad-web-design/` and `/rawalpindi-web-design/`, each with
  its own `Service` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` markup and local area lists.
- Title tags and meta descriptions rewritten around "Islamabad" and "Rawalpindi".
- NAP (name, address, phone) in the footer of every page, consistent and crawlable,
  with `tel:` links so a phone visitor can tap to call.
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.

## 1. Google Business Profile — do this first

Without this you cannot appear in the map pack, which is where most of the local
clicks go.

1. Create the profile at business.google.com as **Designs Redefined**.
2. Primary category: **Website designer**. Secondary: *Marketing agency*,
   *Internet marketing service*, *Graphic designer*.
3. If you work from home or a shared desk, set it up as a **service-area business**:
   hide the street address, set the service area to Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
   Do not publish a fake address, a flagged listing is worse than no listing.
4. Verification is usually video in Pakistan. Record the video with your laptop open
   on the real site, any signage, and the street outside. Have it ready before you start.
5. Fill every field: phone `+92 337 484 1818`, site `https://designsredefined.com/`,
   hours Mon-Sat 10:00-19:00, opening date, services (one per service on the site),
   and a description that uses "Islamabad" and "Rawalpindi" naturally in the first sentence.
6. Upload at least 10 photos: workspace, team, and screenshots of the three live client
   sites. Add new ones monthly, Google reads activity as a freshness signal.

**Keep the NAP identical everywhere** — same spelling of the business name, same phone
format, same city. Inconsistency is the single most common reason local listings
underperform.

## 2. Reviews — the actual ranking lever

You have 50+ projects shipped and, as far as the site shows, zero public reviews.
That is the biggest gap between you and whoever currently ranks.

- Target: **10 Google reviews in the first 60 days**, then 2-4 per month, forever.
- Ask by WhatsApp the day a site goes live, while the client is happy. Send the direct
  review link (GBP gives you one) so it is two taps, not a search.
- Ask them to mention the city and the service in their own words: "great web design,
  Islamabad" in a real sentence beats a bare five stars for ranking.
- Reply to every review within a day, including bad ones. Replies are public and they
  are read by the next prospect.
- Never buy reviews. Google's Pakistan spam enforcement is aggressive and a suspension
  costs months.

## 3. Local citations — build in this order

Consistent NAP on each. Free unless noted.

**Pakistan-specific (highest value):**
- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Facebook page (you have one — add the full address, hours and services)
- Instagram business profile with the city in the bio
- LinkedIn company page
- OLX Pakistan (services listing)
- Pakistan Business Directory, Pakistan Yellow Pages, Businesslist.pk, Findpk,
  Pakbiz, Hamariweb business directory
- Rozee.pk employer profile (a real signal of a real company)

**Agency directories (for referral traffic as much as SEO):**
- Clutch.co, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, The Manifest

**Local trust:**
- Islamabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry membership, if the budget allows.
  Chamber listings carry weight and get you in the room with the clients you want.

## 4. Keywords worth owning

Grouped by intent. The city pages target the first group; write one blog post or case
study per quarter for the rest.

| Intent | Keywords |
|---|---|
| Core local | web design islamabad, web development islamabad, website designer islamabad, web design company in islamabad, web design rawalpindi |
| Service + city | ecommerce website development islamabad, landing page design islamabad, seo services islamabad, logo design islamabad, digital marketing agency islamabad |
| Price intent | website design cost in pakistan, website price in pakistan, cheap website design islamabad |
| Area level | web design blue area, web designer bahria town, website developer dha islamabad, web design saddar rawalpindi |
| Industry + city | website for clinic islamabad, real estate website pakistan, restaurant website islamabad, law firm website pakistan |

"Website design cost in Pakistan" is high-volume and under-served — most agencies hide
pricing. A straight, honest page answering it would rank and would pre-qualify leads.

## 5. Content that earns local links

Thin "we are the best agency in Islamabad" pages do nothing. These do:

- **Case study per client, named by city.** "How Al-Qanoon E-Stamps serves Islamabad and
  Rawalpindi customers online." You already have three live sites, write all three up.
- **A real pricing page in PKR.** Ranges, not exact numbers, with what changes the price.
- **"What a website should cost in Pakistan in 2026"** — the post everyone searches for
  and nobody writes honestly.
- **Local business teardowns.** Pick a visible Islamabad business with a weak site, show
  what you would change. These get shared and they are a sales pitch that does not look
  like one.

## 6. Technical follow-ups not done here

- `assets/og.jpg` must exist and be 1200x630. The meta tags reference it; if it 404s,
  every WhatsApp and Facebook share of your site renders as a grey box. **Check this first.**
- Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and verify the domain.
- Set up Search Console + a lightweight analytics script, and watch the
  "Performance > Search results" report filtered to Pakistan.
- Run PageSpeed Insights on mobile. The homepage embeds live client sites in iframes on
  demand, which is already the right call; confirm nothing else is blocking first paint.
- Consider `hreflang` only if you later publish Urdu pages. Not needed today.

## 7. Paid, if you want leads this month

SEO takes months. If you need enquiries now:

- Google Search ads on the price-intent keywords above, geo-targeted to Islamabad and
  Rawalpindi, pointed at the city landing pages in this repo (they are built for it).
- Facebook and Instagram ads geo-fenced to the twin cities, creative showing a
  before/after of a real client site.
- Budget in PKR, start small, and judge on WhatsApp enquiries, not clicks.

## Honest expectations

- Weeks 1-2: GBP live and verified, citations submitted, site changes deployed.
- Weeks 3-8: first reviews land, map pack impressions start showing in GBP insights.
- Months 3-6: city pages begin ranking for the longer-tail terms. Core terms like
  "web design islamabad" are competitive and take longer, and reviews decide them.

Review progress monthly in Search Console and GBP insights. If the map pack is not
moving, the answer is almost always more reviews, not more pages.
