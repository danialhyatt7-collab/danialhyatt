The honest part

What moves the price up

Things that cost more

  • Page count. Every page past the package is designed, not duplicated.
  • Selling online. Payments, delivery and stock management are real engineering.
  • Content. If you have no copy or photos yet, writing and sourcing them takes time.
  • Bookings, portals, logins. Anything with accounts or scheduling is custom work.
  • Urdu or a second language. A second language is a second set of everything.
  • Speed. Rush timelines mean pushing other work aside, so they carry a premium.

Things not included in any package

  • Domain. Around PKR 3,000 per year, billed at cost. You own it, in your name.
  • Hosting. PKR 8,000 to 25,000 per year depending on the site, billed at cost.
  • Photography and video. We can arrange a local shoot and quote it separately.
  • Ad budget. If we run ads, Facebook and Google are paid directly by you.

Pricing questions

How much does a website cost in Pakistan?

A single landing page starts at PKR 45,000. A 5 to 8 page business website starts at PKR 120,000. An online store with checkout starts at PKR 250,000. Where you land in the range depends on page count, whether you are selling online, and how much content work is involved.

Why are you more expensive than a PKR 25,000 website?

Because those are templates with your logo dropped in. We build by hand, so the site is designed around how your business actually sells, loads in under two seconds, and is built to rank. A cheap template usually gets replaced within a year, which makes it the more expensive option in the end.

How do payments work?

Fifty percent to start, fifty percent on launch, by bank transfer in PKR. You get a written scope and quote before any money changes hands.

Do you offer instalments?

For projects over PKR 200,000 we can split payment across three or four milestones tied to delivery stages. Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do the prices include GST?

Prices are quoted exclusive of tax. If your business requires a tax invoice, tell us up front and we will show it on the quote.

What if I need changes after launch?

The business and store packages include 30 days of free fixes after launch. After that, small changes are covered by the site care plan at PKR 12,000 per month, or billed as needed.

Who owns the site when it is done?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, the site is yours, and you are not locked into us for hosting or care. We would rather you stayed because the work is good.
